Happy birthday, Tehilahhhhh! ❤️

I honestly don't even know where to begin because there is so much I want to say to you. First, I need you to know just how much you mean to me. I don't think I say this enough, but I genuinely value you, our friendship, the person you are and the privilege of having you in my life. You're one of those people I look back on and feel genuinely grateful that our paths crossed. And after all these years, I still find so many reasons to be proud of you.

I actually went to Google Photos today and searched your name, just to go through some of our pictures and memories. And honestly, it was such a beautiful reminder of how much history we have and how many versions of ourselves we've seen each other become. It made me appreciate our friendship even more. We've come a long way, and I hope you never doubt how special you are to me.

I am so incredibly proud of you, Tehilah. Not just for the things you've achieved or the things people can see, but for the person you've grown into. Your heart, your kindness, your resilience, the way you show up for people, the way you check up on me even when I haven't particularly been at my best, the random compliments, the encouragement and all the little things you probably don't even realise mean so much. You're genuinely one of the best people anyone could ask to have in their corner, and I hope life gives you a thousand reasons to feel as appreciated as you make other people feel.

And I want you to remember something as you enter this new chapter: your story is nowhere near finished. There is so much more waiting for you. Every single dream you've carried, every goal you've set, every version of yourself you've imagined becoming, I genuinely believe you'll get there. Please don't ever allow the timing of your journey to make you question the destination. Sometimes life takes us through routes we never anticipated, but that doesn't mean we're lost. These moments are commas, Tehilah, not full stops. Your dreams are still valid, your aspirations are still within reach, and there is absolutely nothing about where you are today that determines how far you can go tomorrow.

I hope you never feel pressured to have everything figured out. I hope you give yourself the same grace you so freely extend to other people. I hope you recognise that even on days when you don't feel like you're making much progress, there is still so much about you worth celebrating. You are enough, even in the in-between seasons. And you deserve good things, not just someday when everything finally falls into place, but right now, too.

I am rooting for you, always. I want to see you win, I want to see you become everything you've ever dreamed of becoming, and I want to be around to witness so many of those beautiful moments. I hope this new year brings you genuine happiness, answered prayers, beautiful opportunities, unexpected blessings, good health, peace of mind and the kind of joy that doesn't need to be explained.

And I'm sorry this message is coming later than it should have. This week has honestly been a lot, and somehow, for the first time, I didn't get my usual birthday message ready ahead of time. Snapchat had to remind me, and I felt terrible about that. You deserved to be celebrated from the very first minute of your birthday. Please forgive me.

But regardless of the timing of this message, never mistake a late birthday text for a diminished place in my life. You matter to me, and you always will.

Happy birthday once again, my dear Tehilah. May life be exceptionally kind to you. May the things you've quietly prayed for find their way to you. May you never run out of reasons to smile, and may this next chapter be everything and so much more than you have hoped for.

I love and appreciate you. ❤️
